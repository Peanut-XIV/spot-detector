Filter by convexity

Convexity is another dimensionless value associated with closed 2D shapes, and it can be used to filter shapes. It is defined as the ratio between a shape's area and the area of it's convex hull.

The minimum bound is inclusive and the maximum bound is exclusive.


an example of a shape's convex hull

For a 2D shape, its convex hull can be thought of as a rubber band going around it, following the contour of the shape as long as there isn't a recess.
