Filtering by inertia

SimpleBlobDetector allows to distinguish different shapes of spots with it's minimum to maximum inertia ratio.

What is inertia?

Inertia has a broad definition, but it is linked to the physical notion of moment of inertia of an object. In our case, it measures how "packed" the shape is along an axis passing by it's center of mass:
The closer a shape is to an axis, the lower its inertia is along the axis. This also means that the more spread-out the shape is relative to the axis, the higher its inertia is.

Also, it is possible to compute the angle of the axis minimizing and maximizing the inertia of a given shape. The calculation is complex but can be found following this link .

To be consise, the ratio of minimum to maximum inertia of a shape give us information on how squished a shape is. A line like object will have an inertia ratio close to zero whereas a circle will have an inertia of 1.

As for other parameters, the minimum bound is inclusive and the maximum bound is exclusive.
